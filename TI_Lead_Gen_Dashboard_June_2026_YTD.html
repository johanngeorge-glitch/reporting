Each month's new-lead cohort and the share that reached SQL. Volume has hovered in the high-20s to high-40s all year (Jan 47 → July 29), but SQL rate has swung hard: it peaked at 81.6% in May then fell back to 44.8% in July — the sharpest single-month drop in the window, back near Jan/Mar's ~43% floor. July's flat lead volume masks a real quality pullback, not just a slow month.
