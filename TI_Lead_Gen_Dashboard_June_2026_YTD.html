Actionable Insights & Learnings
watch
LinkedIn Paid Social volume is back up since we turned the Ads on in the LifeSciences account. To start running awareness/net new ads on the TI account.
watch
Google Ads converted 6 leads in July (much like May), with a high number of leads coming from the CTMS and eCTD campaigns. Optimizations have been made on the account and we will be scaling back spend and channeling money into the high converting campaigns.
good
Google Ads UTM tracking is clean this month. All 6 of July's Google Ads leads carry verifiable utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc tags (or a gclid) on their own landing page — the manual confirmed-leads list wasn't even needed to classify any of them this month. Their landing pages (CTMS, eCTD, quality-management-system, tmf-services) directly corroborate the campaign mix noted above.
watch
SQL rate has fallen sharply for two months running. May 66.0% → Jun 71.0% → Jul 34.3% — July's rate is now the lowest of the year even with July's lead volume having risen, not fallen. This is a quality/mix story, not a volume story.
good
Sales assignment coverage is 100% this month. All 7 of July's leads scoring 100–149 already have an owner in the CRM — no gaps to route.
watch
Passive-to-active conversion is still near zero, lifetime. Across the full available history, gated-resource-origin leads convert to a contact form only 3.9% of the time (3 of 77), and LinkedIn-ad-origin leads have converted 0.0% of the time (0 of 109). This mirrors every prior month; the lack of a structured nurture program remains the most consistent gap in the funnel.
